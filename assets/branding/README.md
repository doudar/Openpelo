# Buy Me a Coffee logo

`buy_me_a_coffee.png` is the unmodified `bmc-logo.png` from the official
[Buy Me a Coffee brand kit](https://buymeacoffee.com/brand), downloaded 2026-11-01
from the Logo & Graphics download linked there. The logo belongs to Buy Me a
Coffee and is used to link to https://buymeacoffee.com/anthonydouv.

The supplied white background keeps the original logo readable on both light
and dark app surfaces. Render with its original square aspect ratio; do not tint.
